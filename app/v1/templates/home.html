Demo flow for v1

For this prototype, UniBrain runs entirely on dummy data modeled after publicly available LIU information, ready to plug into real systems later.

  1. Ingest public data. Programs, majors, requirements, and key office contacts from the LIU website.
  2. Build the UniBrain Graph. Connect majors → requirements → courses → departments → offices.
  3. Expose UniBrain Assist. A simple chat UI where students can ask questions about majors and requirements.
  4. Future: plug into real systems. Integrate safely with SIS, LMS, and advising once IT approves.
